## 5. Design & tone guidance

* **Visual style:** Professional, clean, modern. No flashy elements. Neutral colours (dark blue/grey/white), strong typography.
* **Tone of voice:** Confident, precise, formal but approachable. Emphasise reliability and clarity, not aggressive marketing.
* **Languages:** English as primary UI/content. Structure content so that a French version can be added later (mirrored pages).
* **Imagery:** Kigali city/business environment, court buildings, meeting rooms, documents and signatures. Avoid cliché “gavel and scales” stock photos where possible.
* **UX priorities:**
  * Very clear navigation to practice areas and Notary page.
  * Prominent contact options (phone, email, form) on every page.
  * Fast loading and fully responsive for mobile users in Rwanda.
